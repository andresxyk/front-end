CSS came late to the TLA party. It competes with Christ Saves Souls and Certified Semen Services, but Cascading Style Sheets is more than a religion or animal husbandry commodity: much more. It is a way out of the bloated, murky HTML generation and maintenance business. It is a way to cut the transmission times of your screens to perhaps 30% what they are now, depending on how bad you've been. It is a way to stop using HTML tables except for train schedule-style tabular data. No more font tags, no more spacer GIFs, no more GIFs of text. And still look good - better even than with all the painful crufty techniques just mentioned.
